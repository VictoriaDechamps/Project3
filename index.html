Los Angeles County Parks and Open Space

This interactive map displays parks and open spaces around LA County. Click a park to discover its acreage, access, and owner. The data is from the LA County parks and open space feature service. From the sample, I changed the layer being shown in the map from canopy/carbon information, to parks and open space information. I removed the legend, changed the pop-up, added a search feature, changed the basemap, as well as the zoom settings.
